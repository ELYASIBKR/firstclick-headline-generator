Three checks before you use a SaaS first screen.

Should the headline lead with a feature or an outcome?

Lead with the job a buyer recognizes, then name the product category if it would otherwise be unclear. “Scheduling software for field-service teams” is easier to place than a broad promise to transform operations.

Can the button say “Start free”?

Only if the next step really offers a free start. Check the required account, card, and trial terms before making that promise; a demo-request form is not a free trial.

What proof belongs near the first CTA?

Use one verifiable detail that resolves a likely question: a real integration, a documented workflow, a clear trial term, or an accurately labeled sample. Do not invent customer logos, usage counts, savings, or speed claims.
