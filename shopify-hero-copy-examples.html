Free Shopify copy examples

Three homepage heroes that say what the store sells.

These are fictional examples, not claims about real stores or results. Use the pattern, then replace every product, audience, delivery, and proof detail with facts your store can support.

Example 1 · Product + use case

For a refillable-cleaning store

Refillable cleaning essentials for everyday messes.

Choose the bottles and refills that fit your routine. Check ingredients and delivery options before you order.

Primary CTA: Shop cleaning refills

Why it works: The first line names the product category and use case. The CTA leads to a matching collection, not an undefined “Learn more.”

Example 2 · Audience + product

For a children's rainwear store

Rainwear for kids who still want to play outside.

Browse waterproof jackets and boots by size. Check each product page for fit, materials, and care details.

Primary CTA: Explore kids' rainwear

Why it works: The audience and product are clear without an unverifiable superlative. Fit questions are answered at the point of choice.

Example 3 · Product + decision aid

For a loose-leaf tea store

Loose-leaf tea for your next quiet cup.

Find a blend by flavour and caffeine preference. Each tea page shows ingredients and brewing guidance.

Primary CTA: Find your tea

Why it works: The supporting line gives shoppers a way to choose, and the CTA promises a specific next step.
