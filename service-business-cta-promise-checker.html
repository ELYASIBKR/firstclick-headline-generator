The difference between six common destinations

Book an appointment should lead to a flow where the visitor can actually confirm a slot. If they can only browse dates, “Check available times” is more exact.
Get a quote should lead to an estimate-request path. Do not promise an instant price if someone must review the request first.
Contact us can fit a general message form. A phone link should instead say “Call [business name]” or “Call us” and work as a tel: link.
See services and pricing fits a list page. If prices are not shown, use “Explore services” rather than implying visitors will see prices.
